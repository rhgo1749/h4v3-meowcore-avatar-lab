# Vision

> Meowcore should be recognizable from one screenshot, one silhouette, or one second of animation.

## Product goal

The project exists to build a **Live2D Meowcore avatar** with a bold cartoon identity rather than a conventional anime-VTuber look.

Desired impression:

- clear outer contour
- compact, readable shapes
- flat graphic color treatment
- expressive face
- exaggerated reaction poses
- rhythm-aware timing
- deliberate holds and snaps
- minimal unnecessary secondary physics

## What success looks like

A successful Meowcore avatar should remain recognizable when:

1. reduced to a black silhouette,
2. shown at chat/stream overlay size,
3. paused on a reaction frame,
4. animated for only one second,
5. rendered without elaborate shading or effects.

## Identity before rig convenience

The project should not drift toward generic Live2D conventions merely because they are easier to rig. Head rotation, physics, hair motion, perspective and deformation exist only when they improve the cartoon.

Large reactions may use intentionally discrete poses, stepped motion, swaps, squash/stretch or narrow parameter ranges instead of forcing continuous pseudo-3D rotation.

## Inspiration policy

Rhythm games, limited animation, mascot art, editorial cartooning and graphic animation may be studied for general principles such as silhouette, timing, spacing and readability.

Do not copy an existing character, asset, exact pose set, costume, face construction or distinctive protected visual design. The destination is a visual language people can recognize as **Meowcore**, not as an imitation of a reference work.
